Payments

Everything happens online.

Review your finished site, sign the handoff agreement, and pay — all remotely, no in-person meeting required.

New builds are temporarily paused. We're finishing setup on secure online payment and digital sign-off. Once that's live, projects will move start to finish without needing to meet in person. Check back soon, or use the contact form to get on the list.

How it works

  1. You submit your project through the contact form, and we scope the build.
  2. We build the site (and chatbot, if included) and keep you updated along the way.
  3. Once it's ready, you review the finished site remotely.
  4. You sign a short handoff agreement online — no meeting, no printing anything.
  5. You pay securely online, and the project (and your domain) is yours.

Your domain, in your name

Every project includes registering your domain directly under your own account with the registrar — not ours. That means you own it outright, you're billed directly for the yearly renewal, and there's no ongoing dependency on G0atX to keep your site online. Once a project is delivered, we don't retain access to your site, hosting, or domain.

Sign your handoff agreement

There's no link to click here yet — once your project is ready, we'll personally send you a secure email from our e-signature provider to review and sign your handoff agreement. No account or app needed on your end.

Note: that email comes from a third-party e-signature service, not from us directly. To produce a valid signed record, that service collects your name, email address, IP address, and a signing timestamp — separately from anything you enter on this website.
