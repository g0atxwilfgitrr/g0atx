Payments

We meet in person before anyone pays.

No online checkout, no card entry on this site. You see the finished work, agree to the terms, and pay in person — on your terms, at your shop.

Online payment isn't available. We don't collect card or online payments through this website. Before any payment, you'll need to review and agree to our policies, and we'll meet in person to walk through your finished site together.

How it works

  1. You submit your project through the contact form, and we scope the build.
  2. We review our policies together — privacy, terms, and refunds — so everything is clear upfront.
  3. We build the site (and chatbot, if included) and keep you updated along the way.
  4. Once it's ready, we come to your shop in person to show you the finished website.
  5. If it looks good, you sign off and pay on the spot — check, cash, Cash App, or Venmo.

Accepted payment methods (in person only)

Check Cash Cash App Venmo

Online payments (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay) are not currently accepted. Everything is handled face-to-face after you've reviewed the finished site.
