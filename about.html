About G0atX

Built for founders who want more than a template.

G0atX is a small, hands-on studio. We combine branding, UX, and engineering into one clean system — so your website, your chatbot, and the way visitors get in touch all work off the same brand and the same logic.

Our approach

We start with your brand — your name, your voice, your pricing, the way you actually talk to clients. From there we design navigation, page flow, and chatbot logic that match how your visitors think and search, not a generic template that happens to have your logo on it.

Every build is iterative. You see drafts as we go, not a single reveal at the end, so nothing about the final site is a surprise.

What makes us different

  • Iterative, collaborative build process — you're involved the whole way
  • Misspelling-tolerant chatbot logic that actually understands visitors
  • Business-ready pages: portfolio, contact, policies, and payments
  • No in-person meeting required — review, sign, and pay online

How a project runs, start to finish

The same process behind every build, whether it's a single branded site or the full system.

1. You tell us about the project

Business details, budget range, and what you need — through the contact form, in a couple of minutes.

2. We design and build

Branding, pages, and chatbot logic come together. You see progress and give feedback as we go.

3. You review and sign off remotely

Before anything is final, you review the finished site, sign a short handoff agreement online, and pay — no meeting required.
